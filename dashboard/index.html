A / THE VALUATION

Value anatomy

LOWERS VALUEBASERAISES VALUE
    HELD-OUT PEER SPREAD · P10–P90

    Translates errors on traded-token peers to this estimate. It is not a launch forecast or a confidence interval.

    Model specification & limits

    G = 7d change / 25%, clipped to −1…1. A = log2(1 + tracked years) − 1, capped at eight years. Missing growth or an unusable date is neutral. Values are rounded to whole USD and bounded to $1…$100T.

    Bounded positive slopes are assumptions, not causal findings. Huber loss downweights outliers; ridge regularization shrinks slopes toward priors. Tokens are deduplicated by Gecko ID, then ticker, keeping the largest-TVL record. Custody, bridges and token wrappers are excluded from calibration. Category peer counts are context, not separate category models.
